Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the DD HRMS application and website created by DDIPL for enterprise Human Resource Management System (HRMS) operations. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Key Summary: DD HRMS is an internal enterprise application. We collect first-party data (such as workplace GPS location, operational photos, and push notification tokens) strictly to perform workplace HRMS functions (attendance check-in, visit verification, field task management, and push alerts). We do NOT track users across apps or websites, do NOT collect advertising identifiers (IDFA), do NOT serve advertisements, and do NOT share data with ad networks or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide core HR management and workforce operation services to your employer:

  • Account & Identity Data: User account credentials (email address, password hash), employee ID, full name, job title, assigned role/permissions, and profile photo.
  • GPS Location Data: Precise device location (latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, altitude, heading) requested via standard location permissions. Location is recorded during:
    • Employee attendance check-in and check-out
    • Client visit check-in verification
    • On-duty live field service representative tracking while active on duty
  • Push Notification & Device Information: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens, device platform (iOS / Android), OS version, app version, and device model name used to route operational notifications.
  • Photos & Camera Content: Photos captured via the device camera or uploaded from the photo library, including:
    • Attendance check-in selfies (captured for workplace identity verification)
    • Client visit proof photos and field task completion attachments
    • Expense claim receipt attachments
  • Workplace Operations Data: Shift schedules, leave requests, expense claims, field task logs, and client visit reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for workplace HR management and field operation purposes, including:

  • Authenticating your account and managing user session security.
  • Verifying workplace attendance check-ins, check-outs, and geofencing compliance.
  • Verifying client visits and managing field sales/service duty logs.
  • Delivering operational push notifications (such as shift reminders, leave request approvals, attendance alerts, and field task assignments).
  • Processing payroll, expense reimbursements, and leave applications.

3. Data Transmission & Storage

  • Backend Server Transmission: Account credentials, location coordinates, photos, and operational logs are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to our backend servers (https://hrms.ddipl.org).
  • On-Device Local Storage: Authentication session tokens (JWT access and refresh tokens) and user preferences are stored securely in encrypted local device storage (MMKV).
  • Push Notification Provider: FCM push tokens are processed through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging solely to deliver operational push notifications to your device.

4. No Tracking, No Advertising & ATT Compliance

  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do NOT track users across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No IDFA / Advertising IDs: We do NOT collect or access Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), Google Advertising ID, or any device tracking identifiers.
  • No Advertising SDKs: The Application contains no advertising frameworks, ad measurement tools, or ad networks.
  • No Analytics Tracking SDKs: The Application contains no third-party user behavior analytics SDKs (e.g., no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, or Sentry).
  • No Third-Party Data Selling: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to ad networks, data brokers, or commercial third parties.
  • AppTrackingTransparency (ATT): Because the Application does NOT perform tracking under Apple's AppTrackingTransparency definition, ATT authorization is not required or implemented.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data during transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and storage. Access to administrative data is restricted to authorized HR administrators and managers assigned by your employer.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your personal data is retained for the duration of your employment or as required by your employer's corporate governance, HR policies, and applicable legal regulations. Account deactivation or data deletion requests must be initiated through your company's HR administrator.

7. Your Rights & Permission Controls

You can manage permissions for Location, Camera, Photo Library, and Push Notifications at any time through your device's Settings. Please note that revoking permissions may disable related features (for example, revoking Location permission will prevent automated location verification during attendance check-in).

You have the right to inspect and request updates to your profile information by contacting your HR department.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the Application, please contact your company's HR administrator directly.
